	==== Boogle – How to Play ====

Boogle is a word search game played on a grid of letters
generated by the program. Your goal is to find and enter
as many valid words as possible.

Words can be formed using any letters on the board. They
do not need to be connected in a straight line. However,
each letter can only be used once per word, and the same
word cannot be entered more than once.

There is no minimum word length, so even short words count.
Each letter in a word is worth 1 point, meaning longer words
will earn you more points.

Try to maximize your total score by finding as many unique
words as possible.
